fn eval_dyn(&self, _alpha: f64, _output: &mut Vec<DynItem>)

Evaluate this node’s content at its own normalized progress alpha, pushing the resulting erased items into output.

Leaves evaluate/project at alpha; containers remap alpha into their content coordinates and recurse into their active children.
