Enum FontStyle 

pub enum FontStyle {
    Normal,
    Italic,
    Oblique,
}
Available on crate feature typst only.
Expand description

The style of a font.

Variants§

§

Normal

The default, typically upright style.

§

Italic

A cursive style with custom letterform.

§

Oblique

Just a slanted version of the normal style.
